This button forces a full re-authentication and triggers a sync of your Bitwarden vault.

This is primarily useful for two scenarios:

  • You have updated the secret content of your API Key or Master Password credential in Jenkins (without changing its ID), and you need the plugin to reauthenticate.
  • You have added, renamed, or deleted items in your Bitwarden vault and want the changes to appear in Jenkins without waiting for the next automatic refresh.

Note on Saving: If you change the other settings for this plugin, clicking Save or Apply will automatically trigger this same reload process. You do not need to click this button after saving changes.
